# The Aegis’s Oppai · XC2S2 Edition — v0.1.9

**Clearer updates.** There were three "Update" buttons for two different things. Now:

- **One notice** at the top, only when there is something to update, with a row for each: **Launcher** (the program: takes a
  few seconds and restarts by itself) and **Game** (the port: builds again for a few minutes; saves and settings are kept),
  each with its versions, its notes and its own button. When both are there, the launcher goes first.
- The button next to **Play** is **"Update game"** and appears only when there is a new version of the game; otherwise Play
  takes the whole width.
- The repeated hints (the "is out" pill and the badge on the version selector) are gone.
